Rooted Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Rooted does not collect, store, transmit, or have access to any of your personal data. Rooted is a 100% local-first application. Everything you do in Rooted stays on your device.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect any personal information. No account is required. We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, advertising networks, or cloud servers.

2. Data Storage

All data you create in Rooted — including your Glow balance, unlocked sceneries, focus history, Soul Contract text, and app preferences — is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's standard local storage (UserDefaults). We cannot access, read, or recover this data.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. Deleting the Rooted app from your device permanently deletes all associated data. We have no server-side copy to retain or delete.

4. In-App Purchases

Rooted offers a one-time in-app purchase (Rooted Pro) processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs the transaction data.

5. Children's Privacy

Rooted does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected or transmitted, there is no COPPA-related data concern.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a future version of Rooted, the updated date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact
